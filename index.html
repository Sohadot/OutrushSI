01 / THE SHIFT

The outward
shift.

Much of the recent progress in AI has been driven by greater concentrations of data, compute, and model capacity.

As those capabilities are deployed, their impact extends outward. Decisions made. Tasks executed. Systems run. Discoveries reached.

Outrush names that change of direction.
